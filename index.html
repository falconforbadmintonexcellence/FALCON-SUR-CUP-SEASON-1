🏸 Falcon Sur Cup Badminton Tournament – Season 1

15th & 16th October 2026 · Falcon Badminton Academy, Senaya, Sur, Oman

Tournament Info

Welcome

Falcon for Badminton Excellence presents the Falcon Sur Cup – Badminton Tournament, Season 1, held at Falcon Badminton Court, Sur, Oman. Under the theme “Learn to play, train to win,” two days of competition on 15–16 October bring together Men's, Women's, and Kids' events for players of all levels. The tournament is about intense matches, community spirit, and one shared passion for badminton — follow along, support the players, and be part of the game.

About the Organizer

Falcon for Badminton Excellence

Falcon for Badminton Excellence is committed to developing badminton talent and encouraging active lifestyles within the community. Through quality coaching, tournaments, and community programs, we strive to inspire young athletes and provide opportunities for players of all levels to compete and grow. Whether you are a beginner or advanced, we can guide you.

What we offer

  • Training sessions for kids and adults.
  • Separate coaching sessions for ladies.
  • Memberships for every player: individual, group, family, corporate and weekend memberships.

Our facilities

  • Two badminton courts, air-conditioned, with synthetic mats.
  • CCTV surveillance.
  • Table tennis available if you are interested.

Our branches

  • Main Branch: Qurm, Muscat
  • Branch: Sur, Oman

Why we host this tournament

  • Promote badminton in Sur and across Oman.
  • Encourage youth and women to participate in sports.
  • Identify and develop emerging badminton talent.
  • Foster sportsmanship and healthy competition.
  • Build a stronger badminton community.
