Tournament Info

Welcome

Falcon for Badminton Excellence presents the Falcon Sur Cup – Badminton Tournament, Season 1, held at Falcon Badminton Court, Sur, Oman. Under the theme “Learn to play, train to win,” two days of competition on 15–16 October bring together Men's, Women's, and Kids' events for players of all levels. The tournament is about intense matches, community spirit, and one shared passion for badminton — follow along, support the players, and be part of the game.
